THE AVENGERS

Iron Man

Google Iron Man, also known as Tony Stark, is a genius inventor, billionaire, and philanthropist. He creates powerful armored suits equipped with advanced weapons, artificial intelligence, and flight technology. Despite having no superhuman powers, his intelligence, courage, and leadership make him one of the strongest members of the Avengers. He often uses his inventions to protect the world from powerful threats.


Captain America

Captain America, whose real name is Steve Rogers, is a brave and honorable superhero. After receiving the Super Soldier Serum, he gained enhanced strength, speed, agility, and endurance. He carries a nearly indestructible vibranium shield, which he uses for both defense and attack. Captain America is respected for his leadership, determination, and strong sense of justice. Thor
